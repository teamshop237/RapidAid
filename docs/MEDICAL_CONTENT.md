# Medical content governance

No medical protocol is included in this repository yet.

```text
Authoritative source → AI organization → draft → clinical review → clinical approval
→ technical safety/QA → release-owner approval → published pack
```

The Medical Content Assistant may organize source references, transform approved
material into a schema, and flag missing metadata. It may not invent, approve, or
publish instructions. Every published item requires source, reviewer, version,
review date, geographical applicability, language review, and expiry/review date.
